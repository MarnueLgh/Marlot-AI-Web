# Marlot AI Reel - OpenAI-Like Product Demo

## Style Prompt

Vertical product demo reel for Marlot AI, designed with an OpenAI-like sense of restraint without copying the brand. The reel should feel white, quiet, typographic and useful: clear black headlines, minimal product UI, a cursor as a guide, deterministic typewriter moments and subtle push or zoom transitions. Marlot appears through small coral actions only: active checks, the counter, cursor accent, logo and final CTA.

## Colors

- Background: `#FFFFFF`
- Primary text: `#101010`
- Secondary text: `#303030`
- Muted text: `#666666`
- UI surface: `#F7F7F7`
- UI secondary surface: `#EFEFEF`
- UI border: `#EDEDED`
- Marlot accent: `#F25C69`

## Typography

- Display and body: `IBM Plex Sans`
- UI labels, counters and typewriter: `IBM Plex Mono`
- Headlines are large but calm, with clean line height and no decorative letter spacing.

## Copy Strategy

- Jobs to Be Done: Marlot is framed as a way to recover time and reduce repeated work.
- Status-quo bias: the copy says "un flujo, no otro sistema" to make adoption feel low-friction.
- Present bias: the CTA points to an immediate diagnostic instead of a vague transformation.
- Goal-gradient: the diagnostic shows `3/5` signals so progress feels tangible.
- Loss aversion: the closing reframes automation as recovering time before hiring more.

## Motion

- Entrances are quick, precise and restrained.
- Preferred eases: `power2.out`, `power3.out`, `expo.out`.
- Avoid bounce, elastic, heavy wipes or promotional card flips.
- Scene transitions are subtle white push panels that cover the cut without becoming the protagonist.
- Typewriter is deterministic and timeline-owned.
- Final scene may fade out; earlier scenes do not use exit animations.

## What Not To Do

- Do not use warm gradients, halos, decorative orbs or large pastel cards.
- Do not turn coral into a background system; it is only an action signal.
- Do not add narration or music until a licensed asset is provided.
- Do not use jump cuts between scenes.
- Do not add new dependencies or modify the Marlot site outside this isolated video project.
